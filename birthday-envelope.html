Dear Daddy, ❤️

On this very special day, I wish you the happiest of birthdays!

As your daughter, I have learnt so much from you: through your walk with Christ, your character, and the beautiful way you carry yourself with elegance and grace. Each day, you inspire me to be hardworking, honest, and, above all, a true child of God.

My memories with you are ones I could never forget, because you have always been there for me. Through my hardest and darkest times, you stood by my side. When things were difficult, you remained my strength and my comfort. And if I were to be reborn, I would still choose you to be my father—over and over again, in every lifetime, without a second thought!

Your heart is as pure as gold, and your kindness is as endless as the stars in the sky. ✨ Thank you for being my world, my rock, and my shield. Thank you for always providing for me, for making sure that I am well, and for going out of your way to give me beautiful experiences. Thank you for taking me to places around the world and giving me memories that I will cherish for the rest of my life.

Thank you for being my Mr. Barry! ❤️ There is nothing more I could wish for than a man like you: a father who loves me, cares for me genuinely, and continues to show me, through his actions, what it means to be loved wholeheartedly. Honestly, Daddy, what more could I ask for? You are a blessing I will never take for granted!

If there is one thing I hope you never forget, it is this: I love you more than words could ever express, and I will forever be grateful that God chose you to be my father. No matter how old I grow, I will always be your little girl!

P.S. I’m still your stubborn daughter! 🤣🤭💕 Don’t expect that to change anytime soon!

With the fullness of my heart, overflowing with love and gratitude,

Your daughter, Janice. ❤️
